NANOCHAT KEY ACHIEVEMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Train GPT-2 capability (0.256 DCLM CORE score) in ~2.76 hours on 8XH100 for ~$72
Original GPT-2 training cost: $43,000 (2019)
Modern speedup: ~600x cheaper, ~60x faster

Single complexity dial: --depth (4 to 32) auto-configures all hyperparameters
